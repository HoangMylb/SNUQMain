# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: a lightweight React/TypeScript site chosen for a responsive, touch-first birthday experience.

## Users

Trịnh Phương Quyên opens a private birthday gift link on her phone, likely on or near 25 August. The creator needs an experience that makes her feel personally remembered rather than marketed to.

## Product Purpose

A single-page digital birthday gift that unfolds from curiosity to warmth through a personal greeting, a letter, memory placeholders, and one small playful candle interaction.

## Positioning

This is made only for Quyên: the precise name, date, affectionate voice, letter copy, and future photo archive make it unlike a generic birthday template.

## Operating Context

Mobile-first native scrolling at 360–430px wide, with graceful tablet and desktop expansion. The primary interaction is tapping or scrolling through a one-handed, low-friction sequence.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- A scroll-led, single-page experience with no traditional navigation.
- The candle interaction must work by touch and never require a microphone.
- Future images live under `/public/images/quyen/`; placeholders must not depict another person.
- Optional music is user-initiated only; no fake audio file and no autoplay.
- Motion must respect `prefers-reduced-motion` and never obstruct content or native scroll.

## Brand Commitments

The selected visual direction is **Pink Letter**: romantic editorial, cute in small details, soft luxury in composition. Emotional storytelling and typography lead; decoration is secondary. Avoid SaaS visual language, generic templates, uncontrolled hearts/confetti, glassmorphism, and blue-purple AI gradients.

## Evidence on Hand

Confirmed copy, date, recipient name, scenes, and interaction requirements are supplied in the project brief. No personal photographs or audio have been supplied; image slots remain clearly labeled placeholders.

## Product Principles

- Reveal the gift in layers rather than showing everything immediately.
- Make the recipient's name and the letter feel authored, quiet, and central.
- Let each scene earn its place in the emotional sequence: curiosity, surprise, joy, tenderness, warmth.
- Treat mobile as the primary composition, not a scaled desktop page.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Readable mobile type, sufficient contrast, touch-friendly targets, semantic controls, native scrolling, and a reduced-motion experience that preserves all content.
